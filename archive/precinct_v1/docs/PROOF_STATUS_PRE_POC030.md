## Important interpretation

`POC028` is the current-plan baseline, and `POC029` is the accepted historical
and ACS extension. Together they prove a direct, chamber-neutral route from all
20 accepted population products to the applicable House and Senate plans
without a precinct dependency. The earlier fixed-precinct work remains saved
evidence, is not an input to these results, and can now be archived through the
`POC030` retention manifest.
