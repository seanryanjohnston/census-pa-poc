# Accepted coverage and fixed-precinct visual review

`POC023` passed on 2026-08-30. It adds a reusable, tested visual-review layer
over the accepted `POC016`, `POC019`, and `POC021` artifacts.

## Outputs

The generator creates three local, ignored PNGs under `artifacts/poc023/`:

- `year_coverage.png` shows all 20 population products against all 19 even-year
  general-election cutoffs, the fixed precinct target, the applicable Senate
  plan sequence, and the number of products available by each cutoff;
- `decennial_population_by_precinct.png` maps the accepted 1990, 2000, 2010,
  and 2020 estimates on the same population bins; and
- `acs_population_by_precinct.png` maps representative 2005–2009, 2011–2015,
  2016–2020, and 2020–2024 ACS five-year estimates on those same bins.

The coverage matrix preserves the strict result that no cataloged product was
available by the 1990 election cutoff. Counts rise to all 20 products by 2026.
Every population product covers all 9,178 fixed target precincts.

## Reuse and modification

The images are generated by small functions in
`src/census_pa_poc/visual_review.py`; they are not hand-edited. Product
selections, shared bins, labels, colors, canvas layout, input checks, and the
command-line entry point are explicit. Focused tests cover Senate-plan
partition collapse, tolerance failure, bin boundaries, plan runs, and title
fitting.

Reproduce them with the accepted local inputs present:

```bash
.venv/bin/python -m census_pa_poc.visual_review --root .
```

The command writes `input_manifest.json`, `qa_results.json`, and `report.md`
beside the images. The manifest records exact input hashes and links back to
the accepted source-provenance manifests. The generated image hashes are also
recorded in QA. A second full run reproduced all three image hashes exactly.

## Validation result

All eight integration checks pass:

- 20 products × 19 elections = 380 availability rows;
- 114 product/election pairings are available by their cutoff;
- all 19 election-registry rows are represented;
- every row retains `pa_lrc_2021_release_1b_geography` as its fixed target;
- each of the 20 product displays has all 9,178 target IDs;
- population and target-geometry identifiers match exactly;
- the maximum fixed-precinct estimate range across duplicate Senate-plan
  partitions is `0.00003914396029358613`, below the accepted `0.001`-person
  tolerance; and
- all three rendered outputs are present and nonempty.

The deterministic display rule selects the lexicographically first Senate-plan
partition for each product only after the plan-invariance check passes. This
avoids averaging nearly identical partitions or silently relabeling a result.

## Interpretation and limits

These visuals show population allocated to one constant target geography. They
are not maps of the precinct boundaries actually used in 1990–2026, and the
2026 column is not evidence of an actual 2026 precinct snapshot. Historical
decennial values remain area-based models, ACS values retain their documented
support and MOE limitations, and external publication remains subject to the
LRC-derived-artifact licensing decision.
