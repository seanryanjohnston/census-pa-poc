Release & methodology

A complete panel, not a “latest” overwrite.

V2 preserves all 18 even-year election cycles and opens on 2026. Each row identifies its applicable plan and cutoff-safe source products. Values are modeled district estimates, not observations of individual people.

Read before reuse

ACS margins of error are 90% margins and do not include geographic-allocation uncertainty. The 1990 and 2000 socioeconomic estimates have no published cell-level margins. District geometry is intentionally not redistributed in this release while Pennsylvania LRC reuse terms remain unstated.

Source: U.S. Census Bureau and Pennsylvania Legislative Reapportionment Commission. See the release notes and metric calculations.
